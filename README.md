# Exploring Functional Emotion Concepts in Large Language Models

An essay comparing Anthropic's [2026] findings on functional emotion
concepts in LLMs ([Emotion Concepts and their Function in a Large Language Model](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/emotions/index.html)) with Lisa Feldman Barrett's
theory of constructed emotion.

**Core question:** Do the emotion-like representations found in LLMs
fit a constructionist account of emotion better than a "basic emotions" view?

I also look at how RLHF, as described in OpenAI's *Training language
models to follow instructions with human feedback* (Ouyang et al., 2022),
might shape these representations.
